La La Lost You

"While you're out there drinking in LA, I'm sitting here thinking..."

The Deep Meaning: "La La Lost You" explores the bittersweet reality of heartbreak compounded by geographic distance. Set against the backdrop of Los Angeles, it captures the ache of watching an ex thrive in a glamorous new city while you are left behind processing the grief of what used to be.

It touches on emotional detachment as a defense mechanism—trying to play it cool while coming to terms with the fact that someone you once knew intimately has become a distant stranger living a completely separate life.
